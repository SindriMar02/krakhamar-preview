The house

A long, low house under the mountain. The kitchen and the living room stand inside floor to ceiling glass, and the fjord fills it.

High timber ceiling, glass from floor to roof

  1. Kitchen and living room

    A full kitchen and a long island under the high ceiling, open to the living room.

  2. By the fire

    Two armchairs, a fireplace and the wall of glass.

  3. Three bedrooms

    Three bedrooms, for six guests. One of them wrote simply: “The beds are very comfortable.”

  4. Bath and laundry

    One bathroom with a shower. A washing machine and a dryer in their own cupboard.

  5. Outside

    A terrace and a garden with sea and mountain views. Free parking at the house.
